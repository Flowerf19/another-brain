## Analysis of the 2 failures

1. **paired cosine median 0.9808 < 0.99.** 4-bit weight quantization shifts
   embeddings ~2% away from fp32 in absolute cosine. This shift does NOT
   degrade ranking: every retrieval delta (aggregate ≤0.02, per-language
   ≤0.03) passes with wide margin, and q4 matches or beats fp32 on MRR.
2. **q4 nDCG@10 0.838 < 0.85.** Absolute nDCG is corpus-difficulty dependent.
   The VI partition (0.767) drags the macro average; 20/60 VI queries are
   no-diacritic variants, which are intrinsically harder to match against
   diacritic documents. fp32 on the same corpus is 0.851 — barely above the
   gate — and the q4↔fp32 delta is only 0.0133.
